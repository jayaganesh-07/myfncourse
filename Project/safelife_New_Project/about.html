OUR STORY

Insurance built by people who've had to file a claim too.

SafeLife started in 2014 after our founders spent months untangling a family hospitalization claim buried in fine print. We set out to build the policy provider we wished had existed — plain terms, honest premiums, and claims that move at the speed of real life.
